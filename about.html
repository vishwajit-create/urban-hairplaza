Our Story

Welcome to Urban Hairplaza

Located right near the landmark Phanishwar Nath Renu Statue in Simraha, Urban Hairplaza was founded to offer high-quality haircuts and comfortable grooming in Araria, Bihar.

We focus on the essentials: clean sanitized clippers and shears, fresh single-use blades, careful consultations before cutting, and an online appointment system so you never have to waste your day sitting in waiting lines.

For elders, families, or busy professionals, we also provide professional doorstep home visits anywhere in Simraha and Araria.
